Core texts, preparation, and reference courses for dynamic programming and reinforcement learning.

## Core reading

### Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control, Volume I

Dimitri P. Bertsekas. Modeling, the principle of optimality, and classical DP.

[Author's book page](https://www.mit.edu/~dimitrib/dpbook.html)

### Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd edition

Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto. Learning from experience, value functions, and policy optimization.

[Official book page](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262352703/reinforcement-learning/)

### Reinforcement Learning: Theory and Algorithms

Alekh Agarwal, Kianté Brantley, Nan Jiang, Sham M. Kakade, and Wen Sun. Bandits, exploration, and regret analysis.

[Authors' website and free PDF](https://rltheorybook.github.io/)

## Background and preparation {#preparation}

| Background | Where it will be used |
| :--- | :--- |
| Probability | Conditional probability, expectation, and sampled estimates. |
| Linear algebra and calculus | Value equations, approximation, and gradients. |

## RL for language models
