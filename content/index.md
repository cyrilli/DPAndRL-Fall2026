## Course description

This course covers the formulation and applications of sequential decision-making problems, dynamic programming for planning with known models, and reinforcement learning from interaction or historical data. Topics include Bellman equations, value and policy iteration, online planning and search, exploration and regret, function approximation, deep RL, policy gradients, and multi-agent learning. Applications range from inventory and logistics to robotics and language models.

**Time:** Tuesdays, 09:50–12:15 (China Standard Time).\

**Prerequisites:** Probability, linear algebra and calculus, and Python programming. See [preparation and readings](resources.md#preparation).
