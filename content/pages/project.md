<p class="module-number">LAB 01 / PLANNING</p>

## Resource-constrained paths

Find a route while respecting a limited resource such as energy or time. Use the state to track that resource and compare the resulting policy with an unconstrained shortest path.

<p class="module-number">LAB 02 / LEARNING</p>

## Learning for sequential decisions

Train and evaluate a policy on a small dynamic decision problem. Compare against a simple baseline and examine performance across repeated runs.

## Optional direction: RL for language models

A small language-model experiment could examine preference feedback or verifiable rewards. This is a possible extension, not a required assignment. Its feasibility will depend on the available computing resources and the final project brief.

Examples from [Stanford CS224R](https://cs224r.stanford.edu/) and [Berkeley CS285](https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/) provide context for how other courses organize this work.

**Requirements to be announced:** team size, computing resources, deliverables, grading, and submission dates.
