**Lecture 14 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Text generation as a sequential decision problem: reward models, verifiable outcome rewards, RLHF, and GRPO. Compare online RL with direct preference optimization.

## Learning objectives

- Identify the states, actions, rewards, and stopping conditions in a response-generation task.
- Distinguish the trainable policy, reference policy, and reward model.
- Compute group-relative advantages and explain what happens when all rewards in a group are equal.
- Explain how online RL and DPO use different forms of training data.
