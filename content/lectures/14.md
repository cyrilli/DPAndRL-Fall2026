**Lecture 14 · Modern applications**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Text generation as a decision process, preference feedback, verifiable rewards, and an introduction to RLHF and reasoning.

## Learning objectives

- Map language generation to states, actions, policies and rewards.
- Distinguish preference-based feedback from verifiable outcome rewards.
- Explain the role of reward models and regularization in post-training.
