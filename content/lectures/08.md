**Lecture 8 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Use a linear reward model to study exploration. Connect ridge regression, confidence bounds, and optimistic action selection in one regret argument.

## Learning objectives

- Calculate directional uncertainty in a small linear-bandit example.
- Derive an optimistic action rule from a confidence ellipsoid.
- Work through the regret upper bound using a supplied concentration inequality and the elliptical-potential argument.

## Reading

[Improved Algorithms for Linear Stochastic Bandits](https://yasinov.github.io/linear-bandits-nips2011.pdf), Theorems 1–3 and Appendix C; [Bandit Algorithms](https://tor-lattimore.com/downloads/book/book.pdf), Chapters 19–20. The [RL theory resources](../resources.md#rl-theory) support a more detailed reading of the paper.
