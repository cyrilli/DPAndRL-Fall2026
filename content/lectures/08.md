**Lecture 8 · Reinforcement learning**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Learning from sampled experience, exploration versus exploitation, and regret in multi-armed bandits. Compare epsilon-greedy exploration, optimism and Thompson sampling.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish planning with a model from learning through interaction.
- Estimate action values and calculate cumulative regret in a small bandit example.
- Explain the intuition behind upper confidence bounds and Thompson sampling.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, Chapters 1-2; Reinforcement Learning: Theory and Algorithms, selected material on bandits and exploration.
