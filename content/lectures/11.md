**Lecture 11 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Direct policy optimization in a finite-horizon, undiscounted task. Derive REINFORCE, introduce state-dependent baselines, and compute an actor–critic update.

## Learning objectives

- Derive a policy-gradient estimator from trajectory probabilities.
- Explain why subtracting a state-only baseline preserves the expected gradient.
- Compute actor and critic updates with their targets held fixed.
- Distinguish variance reduction from bias introduced by an approximate critic.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, Chapter 13; [Farahmand, Policy Search](https://amfarahmand.github.io/IntroRL/lectures2025/lec06.pdf), selected score-function, baseline, and actor–critic sections.
