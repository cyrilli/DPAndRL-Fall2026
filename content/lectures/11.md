**Lecture 11 · Reinforcement learning**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Cooperation and competition in games, equilibrium concepts, and a first look at learning in Markov games. Introduce centralized training with decentralized execution at a conceptual level.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish cooperative, competitive and mixed-motive interactions.
- Find best responses and an equilibrium in a small matrix game.
- Explain why other learning agents make the environment nonstationary.
- Distinguish centralized training from decentralized execution.

## Reading

Selected lecture notes on matrix games, Markov games and introductory multi-agent reinforcement learning; specific readings will be announced.
