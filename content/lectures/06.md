**Lecture 6 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Monte Carlo and temporal-difference methods for evaluating a fixed policy, followed by Q-learning for control. Use a terminating example for MC/TD, then return to a continuing discounted task for Q-learning.

## Learning objectives

- Compute MC, TD, and Q-learning targets and identify what each estimates.
- Distinguish the policy generating the data from the policy being evaluated or optimized.
- Handle true task termination differently from a cutoff in data collection.
- Explain the exploration and step-size conditions in the discounted tabular Q-learning convergence result.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, §5.1 and §§6.1–6.5; [Farahmand, Learning from a Stream of Data](https://amfarahmand.github.io/IntroRL/lectures2025/lec04.pdf), selected MC, TD, and Q-learning sections.
