**Lecture 6 · Dynamic programming**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Approximate value functions, online search and rollout for problems where exact DP is too costly, followed by a brief introduction to partial observations.

## Learning objectives

- Explain how approximate values reduce the cost of planning.
- Work through a short lookahead search and a rollout using a base policy.
- Recognize when an augmented or belief state is needed under partial observations.

Selected MIT 6.231 lectures on imperfect information and approximate DP.
