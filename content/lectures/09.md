**Lecture 9 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

A worst-case lower bound for linear bandits, followed by exploration in finite-horizon linear MDPs. Use a small example to connect bandit regression and optimism to Bellman backups.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the bandit lower-bound argument using a supplied testing lemma.
- Compute an optimistic backward update in a small linear MDP.
- Identify which bandit proof steps transfer and why fitted continuation values require additional concentration arguments.
- Compare the dependence of MDP regret bounds on dimension, horizon, and sample size.

## Reading

[Bandit Algorithms](https://tor-lattimore.com/downloads/book/book.pdf), §24.1; [Provably Efficient Reinforcement Learning with Linear Function Approximation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05388), the model, Algorithm 1, and Theorem 3.1. Full MDP concentration and lower-bound proofs are optional reading.
