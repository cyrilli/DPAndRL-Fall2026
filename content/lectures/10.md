**Lecture 10 · Reinforcement learning**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Bootstrapping, TD prediction, SARSA and Q-learning in a common tabular environment, with a short comparison of one-step, n-step and Monte Carlo targets.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the TD error and bootstrapping.
- Implement SARSA and Q-learning.
- Compare on-policy and off-policy control.
- Relate n-step returns to the one-step TD and Monte Carlo targets.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, Chapter 6; Chapter 7 for the optional n-step extension.
