**Lecture 10 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Move beyond value tables to fitted Bellman updates and DQN. Trace a small gradient update, then examine replay, target networks, and the training loop.

## Learning objectives

- Form a regression target and compute one value-function update.
- Explain replay and target networks and how they affect training.
- Distinguish true terminal transitions from external time limits.
- Recognize why nonlinear approximation does not inherit tabular Q-learning guarantees.

## Reading

[Farahmand, Value Function Approximation](https://amfarahmand.github.io/IntroRL/lectures2025/lec05.pdf), selected fitted-iteration and semi-gradient sections; [Human-level Control through Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/dqn/DQNNaturePaper.pdf), the loss and Algorithm 1. The [PyTorch DQN tutorial](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/intermediate/reinforcement_q_learning.html) provides an implementation example.
