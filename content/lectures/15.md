**Lecture 15 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Evaluate an LLM training result, then study a small two-player zero-sum game. Connect equilibrium computation to best-response learning through double oracle and policy-space response oracles (PSRO).

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish training reward from independent task success and compare methods at a stated sampling budget.
- Find best responses in a small matrix game.
- Explain how a policy population is expanded using single-agent RL.
- Explain why an equilibrium within a restricted population need not solve the full game.

## Reading

[Scaling Laws for Reward Model Overoptimization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.10760), Figure 1 and §2.1; [A Unified Game-Theoretic Approach to Multiagent Reinforcement Learning](https://mlanctot.info/files/papers/nips17-psro.pdf), §§2–3 and Algorithm 1. Optional background: [Roughgarden’s Algorithmic Game Theory](https://timroughgarden.org/f13/f13.html), Lectures 13 and 17–18.
