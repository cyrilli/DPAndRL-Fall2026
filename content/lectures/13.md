**Lecture 13 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Build on the policy-gradient example to study PPO. Connect the old-policy probability ratio and clipped objective to rollout collection and repeated minibatch updates.

## Learning objectives

- Compute the clipped objective for positive and negative advantages.
- Track the old policy, fixed advantages, and value targets through an update.
- Explain the limits of reusing a rollout batch.
- Explain why clipping is not a hard KL constraint or a guarantee of policy improvement.

## Reading

[Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.06347), §§3 and 5, including Algorithm 1. Review [Lecture 11](11.md).
