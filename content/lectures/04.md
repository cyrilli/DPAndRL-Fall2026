**Lecture 4 · Dynamic programming**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Discounting, stationary policies, Bellman equations, and the intuition behind contraction.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the role of a discount factor.
- Formulate an infinite-horizon discounted MDP.
- Connect finite-horizon recursions to a fixed-point equation.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, Chapter 3; MIT 6.231 infinite-horizon lectures.

## Notes

[Bellman operators and contraction](../notes/bellman-operators.md) develops the fixed-point argument for discounted finite MDPs.
