**Lecture 4 · Tentative**

[Schedule and materials](../schedule.md)

Algorithms for a known, infinite-horizon discounted MDP: policy evaluation, policy improvement, and stopping criteria.

## Learning objectives

- Implement value iteration and policy iteration.
- Prove policy improvement and explain when policy iteration terminates.
- Use a Bellman residual to assess an approximate solution.

## Reading

Sutton and Barto, Chapter 4; [Farahmand, Planning with a Known Model](https://amfarahmand.github.io/IntroRL/lectures2025/lec03.pdf), sections on value iteration and policy iteration.

## Notes

[Bellman operators and contraction](../notes/bellman-operators.md) reviews the discounted fixed-point theory used in this lecture.
